## Why manufacturer photographs are currently held

A manufacturer web page, search result, sales listing, PDF or press release is evidence of a product identity, **not** evidence that a third-party commercial comparison site may re-publish, crop, resize, store or redistribute the imagery. The separate exact-identity and reuse-rights gates apply to all assets, whether the tractor page is published or a private draft. Never hotlink images or show AI-generated imagery as a real model photograph.

## Acceptance checklist for EACH proposed asset

1. Exact model/variant identity: manufacturer product page plus at least three independent identifying cues from the pictured file (badge, styling, source description, year, visible equipment). No rebadging assumption without proof.
2. Legal provenance: photographer/owner, original URL, file-level license URL or explicit written right to publish commercial copies and adaptations; no NC/ND photos for edited/commercial use. Review contradictory rights claims; do not assume image-search reuse.
3. Attribution: required credit line, source link and license/permission terms are associated with the final file; visibly credit the photographer and preserve ShareAlike requirements.
4. Media quality: store an optimized first-party WebP/AVIF or suitable JPEG, define responsive srcset/sizes, natural alt text for ten languages, width/height and a full-screen preview. Don't expose unauthorized third-party source URL as a hotlink.
5. Draft safety: research records for unpublished tractors stay **private**; no model, URL, screenshot, image sitemap or public page leaks from draft data. Do not upload contracts or private email messages to a public GitHub repo; record a non-sensitive rights verification reference only.
6. Evidence and CI: publish model photo only after both the identity and rights gates independently pass and image SEO/attribution tests succeed. Missing rights means an honest placeholder and an outstanding rights request, never fabricated clearance.

No legal advice is provided here; where license terms are unclear, consult the rights holder rather than publish.
