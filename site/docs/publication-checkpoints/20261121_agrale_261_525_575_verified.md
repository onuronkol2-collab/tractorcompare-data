### Data correctness and source-version cautions

- Agrale **525** manufacturer exact product-page says torque **8.5–10.2 kgf.m (83–100 Nm) at 1650 rpm**. This is a RANGE, **not** a single maximum value. Former canonical key `engine_max_torque` was corrected to **`manufacturer_engine_torque_range_at_rpm`** with an explicit Portuguese maker URL, unit retained and measurement note, to avoid false 100 Nm scalar cross-model comparisons.
- Agrale 525 maker product-page says 25 **cv** /18.4kW @2350 rpm. Product-page power is quoted with manufacturer provenance; no inference of specific engine SAE/ISO tested power standard from its short marketing product page.
- Agrale **575 Compact** existing `engine_model='Perkins 1104D'` lacked the producer's exact suffix. Fixed to **`Perkins 1104D-44`** from official model page, before-state retained privately.
- **575 Compact** and **575 Super** manufacturer short product pages say **75 cv**, while the maker's detailed generated technical documents cite **73.4 cv under ISO 14396** and **76.1 cv under SAE J1995** at 2200 rpm. These are **different measurement standards** and must **not** be merged with the short 75 cv category or taken as 75 rated SAE/ISO hp. The three tractors have `tractors.horsepower=NULL`, model_year NULL, drivetrain NULL, and the public model field is labeled **manufacturer-advertised metric cv** rather than a measured common-standard power. Short product-page variants and optional equipment can vary by market/year; do not infer 4WD/cab/weight without edition.
- New gearbox fields: 525 **8F×8R**, 575 Compact **15F×15R**, 575 Super **15F×15R**, directly on the producer's exact product pages. These are new model-specific `tractor_specs` rows with maker source IDs and evidence/verification metadata.
- The supplied online PDFs' embedded *text* showed the manufacturer alternative ISO/SAE figures; attempted web PDF screenshots did not render due cache miss. Treat their visual layout as unverified and do **not** bulk-import tables from them until page render succeeds and variants are checked. No file rights assumption from official imagery.
