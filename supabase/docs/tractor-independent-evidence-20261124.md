## Directly observed new 2026 quality evidence

Durczak and Beba, Journal of Research and Applications in Agricultural Engineering, online 17 November 2026, DOI 10.53502/jraae-242607. Article metadata and abstract: 140 questionnaires, 21 brands, 44 attributes on a seven-point scale; formal brand analysis covers six brands (108 questionnaires), model visualization covers 12 models with at least three respondents each. One author is affiliated with agricultural trade media. Survey collection date, sampling, conflicts and complete methodology must be checked in full text before editorial use. User ratings are not measured breakdown frequency. Do not use this to rank all 21 brands, estimate failure rate, or claim the survey was conducted in 2026 merely because publication was in 2026.

CLIMMAR official release 10 November 2026: survey April–May 2026, ten countries; explicit exclusion of product performance. Dealer relations only. Do not turn into reliability, quality or model-failure rankings. If already recorded in forum editorial evidence, deduplicate rather than rewrite.

## Standing evidence rule

Use independent sources alongside OEM documents even when OEM data exists, when comparable evidence is available. Preserve distinction between original standardized lab test, academic study, editorial field test, dealer survey and owner anecdote. Identify funding, test provider, publication/observation date, model variant, market, measurement method, conditions, sample size and denominator where applicable. A magazine repeating DLG data and DLG report are one measurement, not two. Do not average incompatible ratings, convert PTO power into rated engine power, or infer missing specs. For 2026 reliability/failure content maintain the existing 2026-only requirement; historical technical specifications/tests may be used for correctly identified historical models, not as current failure evidence. Forum posts and video reviews are leads, never incidence rates. Photo rights are separate: no copying editorial images without explicit compatible permission.

At the user's explicit request, do not rely on one research source, translation provider or AI engine. Use the existing AGENTS.md maximum-benefit rule as the primary authority.
